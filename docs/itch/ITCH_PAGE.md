## Description (paste into the page)

**Before Vicmap, someone had to build the map. Today, that's you.**

You're the emergency dispatcher for Burrundara, a fictional suburb built from
real Victorian map data. Calls come in, and you draw each crew's route along
the streets yourself: police, ambulance, fire rescue and SES.

But your map only knows what your crews have seen. Traffic they drive past
lights up in colour, then fades back to grey. Roads close since you last
looked. A route that *looked* clear can stop a crew at a roadblock while the
call's timer keeps running. Redraw fast, or the call gets passed to a
neighbouring council.

- **Draw every route.** Pick a crew, then drag from its station to the scene
  along the roads.
- **The map goes stale.** Colour means fresh information; grey means you're
  guessing.
- **Expect the unexpected.** Hidden roadblocks, crashes needing more than one
  service, house fires and storm damage.
- **Three stages.** The city grows from one junction to all of Burrundara.
- **After-action report.** See how much stale data cost your crews.

Made for the Department of Transport and Planning's *Vicmap: The Race Against
Time* challenge, about why accurate, up-to-date location information matters
when every minute counts.

### How to play

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Select a call | Click its pin or card |
| Choose a crew | "Send" button, or click a station |
| Draw a route | Hold left mouse and drag along the roads; release on the incident |
| Redraw a stuck crew | Press on the crew and drag a new route |
| Undo / clear route | R / Space |
| Pan / zoom | Right-drag / mouse wheel |
| Pause | Esc |
| Mute | M |

Lose three hearts and the run ends. Clear all three stages to win.
Click once to start audio. Best in Chrome, Edge or Firefox on desktop.
